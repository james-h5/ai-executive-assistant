## 3. English

**Why this matters:** Senior English is assessed almost entirely on written analysis and construction of texts under time pressure â€” the Year 10 skill gap is usually essay *structure* and *textual evidence*, not general literacy.

Senior units: Unit 1 Perspectives and texts â†’ Unit 2 Texts and culture â†’ Unit 3 Textual connections â†’ Unit 4 Close study of literary texts.

**Year 10 foundations to build:**
- Essay structure â€” clear thesis, topic sentences, paragraph-level argument, integrating quotes as evidence
- Textual analysis â€” identifying and explaining author's techniques (language, structure, perspective) rather than just plot summary
- The three writing modes senior English rotates through: analytical (essays about texts), persuasive (opinion/argument), imaginative (creative writing) â€” worth practising all three in Year 10, not just essays
