# Italian Primer

## Pronunciation
- `c` before e/i = "ch" sound (ciao = chow); before a/o/u = "k" sound (casa = kah-za)
- `ch` = always "k" sound (chi = kee)
- `gli` = "ly" sound (famiglia = fa-mee-lya)
- `gn` = "ny" sound (bagno = ba-nyo)
- Vowels are pure and consistent, like Spanish

## Numbers 1–20
uno, due, tre, quattro, cinque, sei, sette, otto, nove, dieci, undici, dodici, tredici, quattordici, quindici, sedici, diciassette, diciotto, diciannove, venti

## Core Grammar
- Nouns are gendered: masculine usually ends in "-o" (il libro), feminine in "-a" (la casa)
- Plurals: masculine "-o" → "-i", feminine "-a" → "-e"
- Verbs conjugate by pronoun — three families: -are, -ere, -ire
- "Tu" is informal you, "Lei" is formal/polite you — use "Lei" with strangers/older people
