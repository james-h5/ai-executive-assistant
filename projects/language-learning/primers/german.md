# German Primer

## Pronunciation
- `w` = "v" sound, `v` = "f" sound (Wasser = vah-ser)
- `z` = "ts" sound (Zeit = tsyte)
- `ei` = "eye" sound, `ie` = "ee" sound (easy to mix these up — remember "ei" sounds like its own name)
- Umlauts (ä, ö, ü) change the vowel sound — ä≈e, ö≈"ur", ü≈pursed "ee"
- All nouns are capitalized, always

## Numbers 1–20
eins, zwei, drei, vier, fünf, sechs, sieben, acht, neun, zehn, elf, zwölf, dreizehn, vierzehn, fünfzehn, sechzehn, siebzehn, achtzehn, neunzehn, zwanzig

## Core Grammar
- Nouns have three genders: der (masculine), die (feminine), das (neuter) — genders often have to be memorized per word
- Plurals vary by word (no single rule) — worth learning each plural alongside the noun
- Verbs conjugate by pronoun; word order puts the verb second in a statement
- "Du" is informal you, "Sie" (capitalized) is formal/polite you — use "Sie" with strangers
