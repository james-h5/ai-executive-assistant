# French Primer

## Pronunciation
- Final consonants are usually silent (e.g. "vous" = voo, not "vouss")
- Nasal vowels: "in/un" (like "an" through the nose), "on" (like "ong"), "an/en" (like "ahn")
- "r" is a soft throat sound, not rolled
- "u" (pursed lips "ew") is different from "ou" (like "oo")

## Numbers 1–20
un, deux, trois, quatre, cinq, six, sept, huit, neuf, dix, onze, douze, treize, quatorze, quinze, seize, dix-sept, dix-huit, dix-neuf, vingt

## Core Grammar
- Nouns are gendered: masculine uses "le", feminine uses "la" (le livre, la table)
- Plurals: add "-s" (usually silent when spoken)
- Verbs conjugate by pronoun — three families: -er, -ir, -re
- "Tu" is informal you, "vous" is formal/polite you (and always plural "you") — use "vous" with strangers
