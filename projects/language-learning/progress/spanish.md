### ⚠️ Do not restart from lesson 1

James's instinct was to "redo and go back to get the most out of this." Blind restarting costs 2–3 weeks of active hours to re-cover ground he mostly has. Do this instead:

**The 20-minute diagnostic** (run it before the first proper session):
1. Re-listen to **Language Transfer lessons 13–15**, answering out loud, pausing properly.
2. If you can produce the structures without the recording prompting you → **continue from lesson 16.**
3. If you're guessing → drop back to lesson 10 and move forward from there.

**Then get the review for free:** re-listen to lessons 1–15 as **stacked** audio — commute, gym, dishes — where you weren't going to spend active hours anyway. You get the consolidation without paying for it twice.

The same rule applies to everything else: review belongs in stacked hours, new ground belongs in active hours.
