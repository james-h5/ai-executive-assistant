## Description

James travels solo and wants to connect with locals rather than staying in the tourist bubble. The goal is to know enough of a language to order food, ask for directions, introduce himself, and have a basic conversation — enough to make someone's day and make a memory.

Use the `/learn-language` skill for practice sessions. Each language has a basics reference in `primers/[language].md` (alphabet/pronunciation, numbers, core grammar, survival phrases) — skim before or between sessions. Vocab learned in sessions also feeds a flashcard-style spaced-repetition review tool: [Layover](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/f4dc26fd-68eb-415e-aeca-e50db982df3a) — seeded with each language's survival phrases, extended after every `/learn-language` session.

## Target Languages

| Language | Why | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Spanish | Anchor Romance language — most widely spoken, most travel utility | High |
| Italian | Bucket list travel destination | High |
| French | Travel staple, widely useful | Medium |
| Portuguese | Closest to Spanish — learned last among Romance languages to avoid mixing them up | Medium |
| German | Europe travel | Medium |

Order (Spanish → Italian → French → Portuguese) sequences the Romance languages together so vocabulary transfers between them; German is unrelated and slots in independently. See `schedule.md` for the monthly rotation.

- Ordering food and drinks
- Asking for directions
- Small talk — compliments, jokes, making friends
- At the bar / social situations
- Shopping and markets
