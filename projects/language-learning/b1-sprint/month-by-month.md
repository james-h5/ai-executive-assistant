# Month by Month

Every month has: **the mission**, **exactly what to consume**, and **the test at the end**. Don't improvise — the sequencing is doing work.

---

## Phase 0 — Machinery
### September 2026 · 12 hrs/wk · Uni Day template

**Mission:** Understand how both languages assemble. Vocabulary is secondary this month — mechanism is everything. By 30 Sept you should be able to *build* simple sentences you've never heard.

**Uni context:** Sem 2 in full swing at 60cp. This is the lightest language month by design.

#### Spanish
| What | Dose |
|---|---|
| **Language Transfer — Complete Spanish** (free, app or YouTube) | Lessons 1–50. ~2/day in Block A. **This is the priority — everything else is secondary.** |
| **Dreaming Spanish** — Superbeginner tier | 20 min/day, Block B |
| **Anki** — Spanish frequency top-1000 deck | 15 new/day, ~10 min reviews |
| Notebook | 3 sentences/day from week 3 |

#### French
| What | Dose |
|---|---|
| **Week 1 only: pronunciation boot camp** | 20 min/day × 5 days. Nasal vowels, liaison, elision, the `r`. Use *Comme une Française* and *Français avec Pierre* pronunciation playlists. **Do not skip this** — French sound-to-spelling is the whole ballgame and fixing it later costs months. |
| **Language Transfer — Introduction to French** (40 lessons, free) | Lessons 1–25 |
| **Alice Ayel** + **Français Immersion** — beginner CI | 20 min/day, Block D |
| **Anki** — French frequency top-1000 | 15 new/day |
| **Journal en français facile** (RFI) | From week 3. 10 min/day. You will understand ~nothing. That's the point — it's ear calibration, not comprehension. |

**End-of-month test:** Record 60 seconds introducing yourself in each language. Save it. You'll play this back in May and it will be genuinely shocking.

### October 2026 · 12→13 hrs/wk

**Mission:** Finish the machinery. Start speaking Spanish. Move from "recognising" to "producing".

#### Spanish
- **Language Transfer** lessons 51–90 — **course complete by ~20 Oct**
- **Dreaming Spanish** — move Superbeginner → Beginner tier, 25 min/day
- **Coffee Break Spanish** Season 1 — 3 episodes/week (English scaffolding, good for structure)
- **Anki** — 15 new/day (running total ~900 words)
- **Reading starts:** *Short Stories in Spanish for Beginners* (Olly Richards) — 1 story/week
- 🔴 **Week 5 (~5 Oct): first italki session.** 30 min, community tutor, ~$8–12. It will be awful. Book the second one before you leave the first.

#### French
- **Language Transfer** lessons 26–40 — **course complete by ~10 Oct**
- **Assimil — French With Ease**: lessons 1–30, one per day, 20 min. Picks up the structural slack after LT ends (LT French is only 40 lessons vs Spanish's 90 — France gets a substitute spine).
- **Kwiziq French** — take the placement test, then 15 kwizzes/day
- **French Comprehensible Input (Lucas)** — A1-labelled videos, 25 min/day
- **Coffee Break French** Season 1 — 3 eps/week
- **Reading:** *Short Stories in French for Beginners* — 1 story/week
- **Dictation** starts — 2×/week, 10 min

**End-of-month test:** Kwiziq French level ≥ A1 complete. Survive a 15-minute Spanish tutor call without switching to English for more than 30 seconds at a time.

## Phase 1 — A1→A2 (through the exam block)
### November 2026 · 13 hrs/wk, dropping to minimums 2–14 Nov

**Mission:** Hold the line through QUT exams, then accelerate hard into the summer.

**Uni context:** 🔴 **Sem 2 exam block ~2–14 Nov.** Drop to the 60-minute minimum-viable day for that fortnight. This is budgeted — it is not falling behind.

#### Both languages, 1–14 Nov (exam mode)
- Anki only + 20 min stacked listening each. Nothing else. No tutors, no reading, no writing.

#### From ~15 Nov (post-exams, ramp up)
**Spanish**
- **Dreaming Spanish** Beginner → late Beginner, 40 min/day
- **Español con Juan** — start. Spanish-only, funny, the ideal A2→B1 bridge. 3 eps/week
- **Notes in Spanish — Inspired Beginners** — 2 eps/week
- Tutor → **2×/week**
- Reading: finish beginner short stories
- ✏️ **Writing starts:** 100 words/week, corrected free on **LangCorrect**

**French**
- 🔴 **Week 9 (~2 Nov, or ~16 Nov given exams): first French italki session.** French output starts later than Spanish deliberately — you need the pronunciation foundation to not cement bad habits.
- **Assimil** lessons 31–60
- **Français Authentique** + **Piece of French** — 3 eps/week
- **Easy French** (street interviews, dual subs) — 2/week. Brutal, real, essential.
- Kwiziq — push toward A2 complete
- ✏️ Writing: 100 words/week, LangCorrect

**End-of-month test:** 20-minute tutor conversation in each, past tense attempted. Anki total ~1,400 words each.

## Phase 2 — The Summer Bank 🔥
### December 2026 – February 2027 · 27 hrs/wk · Summer Day template

> **This phase is 47% of your total hours. Everything else in this plan is support structure for these thirteen weeks.**
>
> Uni load is 24cp (MGB130 + EFB210) against the 60cp you're carrying now. No fight camp, no business push — you said park them. This window does not come back.

### December 2026

**Mission:** A2 → the bottom of B1. Volume, volume, volume.

**Spanish**
- **Dreaming Spanish** Intermediate tier — 60 min/day (the drawings stop, speed increases; it'll feel like a step backwards for a week)
- **Español con Juan** — daily
- **How to Spanish** — 3/week
- **Destinos** (free, learner.org) — the classic 52-episode telenovela course. 3 eps/week. Story pull makes the hours painless.
- Anki: 20 new/day + **start sentence mining** — 5 sentences/day pulled from what you actually watched
- Reading: *Short Stories in Spanish for **Intermediate** Learners*, 20 min/day
- Tutors: **2×/week, 60 min**
- ✏️ 2× 150-word pieces/week

**French**
- **InnerFrench** — 🔴 **start here, this is the centrepiece.** Hugo speaks slowly and clearly at genuine B1 level, free transcripts, and it's the single best bridge to real French. 3 eps/week, each one **twice**: once cold, once with transcript.
- **Journal en français facile** — daily, now *with* transcript comparison
- **Français Immersion / Wandering French** — 40 min/day
- **Assimil** lessons 61–90
- **Extra en français** — 2 eps/week (cheesy sitcom written for learners; do it anyway)
- Anki: 20 new/day + 5 mined sentences
- Reading: French intermediate short stories
- Tutors: **2×/week, 60 min**
- Dictation: 3×/week
- ✏️ 2× 150-word pieces/week

**End-of-month test:** 30-minute conversation, each language, no English. Understand a *Journal en français facile* bulletin at ~50% cold.

### January 2027

**Mission:** Consolidate B1 grammar. Both past tenses, future, conditional, and first contact with the subjunctive.

**Spanish**
- Grammar focus: **preterite vs imperfect** (the thing that separates A2 from B1 in Spanish), then present subjunctive
- Kwiziq Spanish or SpanishDict drills — 25 min/day
- Dreaming Spanish Intermediate + **first native content**: *La Casa de Papel* or *Élite* with **Spanish** subtitles via Language Reactor. 3 eps/week
- **Españolistos** + **Spanish Language Coach (Intermediate)** — daily rotation
- Anki: 20 new/day, sentence mining now the majority of new cards
- Reading: finish intermediate stories → start *El Principito* or a YA novel
- Tutors 2×/week, now **topic-driven** — bring an article, discuss it

**French**
- Grammar focus: **passé composé vs imparfait**, then the future and conditional
- Kwiziq French — push to **B1 in progress**
- InnerFrench 3/week + **Easy French** 3/week
- **First native TV:** *Dix pour cent* (Call My Agent) or *Lupin*, French subs, 3 eps/week
- Reading: *Le Petit Prince*
- Tutors 2×/week, topic-driven
- Dictation 3×/week

**End-of-month test:** Write 200 words in each on "the best trip I've taken" — tutor marks it against B1 descriptors. Anki ~2,000 words each.

### February 2027

**Mission:** Bank the last of the free hours. Sem 1 starts ~22 Feb — after that you're back to squeezed.

- **1–21 Feb: run the Summer Day template at full intensity.** Last big push.
- **22 Feb onward:** drop to Uni Day template. Expect a comprehension dip for a week as volume falls. Normal.
- 🔴 **BOOK THE EXAMS THIS MONTH.** DELF B1 at Alliance Française de Brisbane (May session) and DELE B1 (Brisbane centre, via Instituto Cervantes Sydney, May session). Registration typically closes ~March — see `checkpoints.md`. **Pay the money. This is the single most important action in the whole plan.**
- Both languages: maintain everything from January, add **one podcast episode a day with no transcript at all** — training tolerance for not understanding
- ✏️ Writing shifts to **DELF/DELE format**: 160–180 words, timed 45 min, once per week per language

**End-of-month test — the big one:** Sit a **full past DELF B1 and DELE B1 paper**, untimed, at home. Score them. You want ≥ 40/100. Below 30 in either means that language needs the majority of March.

## Phase 3 — B1 Build
### March 2027 · 18 hrs/wk · Uni Day template

**Mission:** Close the gaps February's mock exposed. Exam format becomes the training method.

**Uni context:** Sem 1 2027, 48cp (EFB344, EFB343, EFB335, BSB399). Heavy but standard load — no overload this time.

**Both languages:**
- Rebalance hours toward whichever mock scored lower — do not split 50/50 out of tidiness
- **Listening:** one exam-style listening exercise per language per day, 15 min. Recordings played twice, then answer without the transcript.
- **Reading:** one exam-style reading passage per language per day
- ✏️ **Writing: 2× timed 45-min pieces per language per week.** Tutor corrects both.
- 🗣️ **Speaking:** tutor sessions shift to DELF/DELE format — guided conversation, information exchange, role play. Tell your tutors explicitly: *"I'm sitting B1 in May, run me through the speaking format."*
- Anki: drop new cards to 10/day, prioritise reviews. Consolidation over acquisition from here.
- Keep one "fun" input source per language so it doesn't become pure exam grind — TV series, football commentary, whatever holds you.

**End-of-month test:** Timed mock speaking section, recorded, each language. Self-score against the DELF B1 grid.

### April 2027 · 18 hrs/wk

**Mission:** Get both languages over the line and make it repeatable.

- **Weekly full mock exam**, alternating languages: Spanish in week 1 and 3, French in week 2 and 4. Timed, all four sections, in one sitting.
- Error log: every mock, write down the three things that cost you the most marks. Drill exactly those for the following week. Nothing else.
- Maintain daily input — do not let mock prep crowd out listening volume
- Tutors: 2× per language per week, all exam format
- Reading: DELF/DELE reading papers + *L'Étranger* (French) / news (Spanish)

**End-of-month test:** 🎯 **Full timed mock in each: target ≥ 50/100 with no section under 10.** This is the go/no-go. Under 40 in a language → sit that one in the November session instead and take the win on the other.

## Phase 4 — Exam Sprint
### May 2027 · 18 hrs/wk

**Mission:** Peak, don't cram.

**Uni context:** ⚠️ Sem 1 assessment is ramping toward June exams. Both peaks collide. This is why the summer had to carry the load — May is taper, not rescue.

- **Weeks 1–2:** two full mocks per language. Drill the error log. Speaking practice daily, even if just 10 minutes talking to yourself.
- **Week 3:** volume down 30%. Light input, keep speaking, sleep properly.
- **Exam week:** morning of each exam — 20 minutes of listening in that language to warm your ear up. Nothing new. Nothing hard.
- 🎯 **Sit DELF B1. Sit DELE B1.**
- After: play the September recordings back. Then book something for the Europe year.
