# Checkpoints, Exams & Tracking

---

## 1. The exams — book these in February

The booked, paid exam is the mechanism that makes the whole plan work. Everything before March is training; from March you're an athlete with a date.

### DELF B1 (French)
- **Where:** Alliance Française de Brisbane — **the only accredited DELF/DALF centre in Queensland**
- **[afbrisbane.com](https://www.afbrisbane.com/french-courses/exams-and-diplomas/delf-and-dalf-examinations/)** · 07 3844 4460
- **Sessions:** May and November. **Target the May 2027 session.**
- **Registration closes before the session** and late enrolments are not processed — 🔴 **call in February 2027** to confirm the exact date and deadline. 2027 dates weren't published as of Sept 2026.
- **Format:** 100 points, 4 sections × 25.
  - Listening 25 min — three recordings, each played **twice**
  - Reading 35–45 min — two texts, everyday material
  - Writing 45 min — ~160–180 words
  - Speaking ~15 min (+10 min prep) — guided conversation, information exchange, role play
- **Pass:** ≥ 50/100 overall **and** ≥ 5/25 in every section. The section minimum is why you can't ignore speaking.
- **Diploma is for life.** No expiry.

### DELE B1 (Spanish)
- **Where:** Instituto Cervantes runs DELE in Sydney, Canberra, Melbourne **and Brisbane**
- **[sidney.cervantes.es](https://sidney.cervantes.es/en/dele_diplomas/dele_registration.htm)** · ic.sydney@cervantes.es · +61 2 9274 9200
- **Sessions:** several per year — target **May 2027**. 🔴 **Confirm the Brisbane date and registration deadline in February.**
- **Format:** same four skills, similar weighting. Pass is a global "Apto"/"No apto" with minimums per group.
- **Diploma is for life.**

### Backup
November 2027 sessions exist for both — but you'll be in Sheffield. If a language isn't ready in April, sit it in Europe instead. Don't sit an exam you'll fail for the sake of the calendar.

## 2. Monthly checkpoints

Last Sunday of every month, 45 minutes. Log results in `progress/spanish.md` and `progress/french.md`.

| # | Test | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The recording** | 3-minute unscripted monologue, each language, on a random topic. Archive every one. Playing September back in May is the best motivation available. |
| 2 | **Kwiziq level %** | French especially. Objective, CEFR-mapped, no self-deception possible. |
| 3 | **Anki mature card count** | Words you actually retain, not words you've seen. |
| 4 | **Cold comprehension test** | One podcast episode you've never heard, no transcript. Estimate % understood. Same podcast series each month so it's comparable. |
| 5 | **Hours logged** | The number that predicts everything. |

### Monthly targets

| Month | Anki mature (each) | Cold comprehension | Kwiziq FR |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sep | 300 | — | — |
| Oct | 600 | 15% | A1 done |
| Nov | 1,000 | 25% | A2 in progress |
| Dec | 1,500 | 40% | A2 done |
| Jan | 2,000 | 55% | B1 in progress |
| Feb | 2,400 | 65% | B1 ~50% |
| Mar | 2,700 | 75% | B1 ~70% |
| Apr | 3,000 | 80% | B1 ~85% |
| May | maintain | 85% | B1 ~90% |

~3,000 known words is roughly the B1 vocabulary threshold. That's the number to steer by.

**Weekly targets:** Sept–Nov 12–13 · Dec–Feb 27 · Mar–May 18

**Running total to hit by 31 May: 750 hours.**

### The three-week rule
If your weekly total is under target three weeks running, **stop and recalibrate that week**. Not in May. The two honest options:

1. **Drop to one language.** Spanish only, at 750 hours, gets you to a comfortable B1+ or even B2. That's a better outcome than two shaky A2s.
2. **Move the deadline.** Sit both in November 2027 from Sheffield instead.

Both are fine. Grinding on while pretending the number will fix itself is not.

## 4. Abort / adjust criteria

| Trigger | Response |
|---|---|
| **End Feb mock < 30/100** in a language | That language gets 65% of March–April hours. The other goes to maintenance. |
| **End Apr mock < 40/100** in a language | Don't sit it in May. Move to November, Sheffield. Take the win on the other. |
| **3 weeks under target** | Recalibrate immediately — drop to one language or move the deadline. |
| **Uni GPA drops below 5.5** | Uni wins. Drop to zero-day minimums for a month. The degree is the actual asset; the exchange depends on it. |
| **Both under 45/100 at end of April** | Sit neither in May. Sit both in November from Europe, where you'll have immersion and a lighter load. Not a failure — a better plan. |

## 5. What "success" actually looks like in June 2027

Not a certificate. Two things:

1. You can walk into a bar in Lyon or Valencia, and have a real conversation with a stranger for an hour — jokes, opinions, plans — without it being an ordeal for either of you.
2. You arrive in Sheffield in September 2027 with a year of cheap flights ahead and no language barrier standing between you and any of it.

The diplomas are just how you prove to yourself you got there.
