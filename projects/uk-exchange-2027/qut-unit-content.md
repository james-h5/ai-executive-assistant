# QUT Unit Content Baseline

Source: QUT unit handbook pages (qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=...), fetched 2026-08-03. All 12cp, School of Electrical Engineering and Robotics unless noted.

## EGB345 — Control and Dynamic Systems
Control systems theory and practical application — modelling, analysis, and design of control systems. MATLAB-based labs.
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB345](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB345)

## EGB348 — Electronics
Characteristics and operation of discrete and integrated-circuit semiconductor devices — diodes, transistors, op-amps. Analog/digital electronic circuit design.
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB348](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB348)

## EGB341 — Energy Supply and Delivery
Electric energy generation and delivery — structure of energy conversion/delivery from power stations through transmission and distribution to customer loads.
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB341](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB341)

## EGB342 — Telecommunications and RF
Analog/digital modulation for single/multi-user communications; RF fundamentals. Time/frequency domain signal analysis, information theory for digital data compression, modulation choice, performance analysis in noisy channels.
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB342](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB342)

## EFB335 — Investments
*School of Economics and Finance.* Investment environment and financial markets, asset classes and valuation techniques, portfolio theory and diversification, risk and return analysis, behavioural finance.
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EFB335](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EFB335)

## Swappable units (from elsewhere in the degree plan — 2026-08-03)
James confirmed these units can be interchanged into 2027 S2 (or matched abroad) instead of one of the 5 above, since none carry a semester-lock. Widens the candidate pool, especially useful where the fixed 5 have no good host-university match.

### EFB343 — Corporate Finance (currently 2027 S1)
*School of Economics and Finance.* Extends earlier finance units into the corporate context — sustainable financial management, working capital management, capital investment decisions, issuance of corporate securities and capital structure, payout policy, mergers and acquisitions, financial restructuring.
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EFB343](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EFB343)

### EFB344 — Risk Management and Derivatives (currently 2029 S1)
*School of Economics and Finance.* Identifying, measuring, and hedging risks associated with exposure to financial securities — focus on market risk (movements in market prices).
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EFB344](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EFB344)

### BSB399 — Real World Ready: Business Capstone (currently 2027 S1)
Advanced analysis of macro/meso/micro business environments to determine critical business priorities, now and future. Team-based assessment (e.g. team culture deck, group presentation) — flagged in `context/degree-plan.md` as possibly carrying a "final year" enrolment rule beyond pure credit-point prereqs, worth confirming before relying on it.
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=BSB399](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=BSB399)

### EGH404 — Research in Engineering Practice (currently 2028 S1)
*School of Electrical Engineering and Robotics.* Builds on fundamental discipline concepts, directly prepares for the final-year engineering project and research-based professional practice. Needs 144cp — already satisfied well before 2027 S2, no prereq blocker to pulling it forward.
Source: [Research in Engineering Practice — Course Hero excerpt](https://www.coursehero.com/file/104368449/QUT-Unit-EGH404-Research-in-Engineering-Practicepdf/) (QUT's own unit page didn't surface directly in search — verify against `qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGH404` before relying on this description)
