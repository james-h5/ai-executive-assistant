# QUT Unit Content Baseline (2027 S2 units)

Source: QUT unit handbook pages (qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=...), fetched 2026-08-03. All 12cp, School of Electrical Engineering and Robotics unless noted.

## EGB345 — Control and Dynamic Systems
Control systems theory and practical application — modelling, analysis, and design of control systems. MATLAB-based labs.
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB345](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB345)

## EGB348 — Electronics
Characteristics and operation of discrete and integrated-circuit semiconductor devices — diodes, transistors, op-amps. Analog/digital electronic circuit design.
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB348](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB348)

## EGB341 — Energy Supply and Delivery
Electric energy generation and delivery — structure of energy conversion/delivery from power stations through transmission and distribution to customer loads.
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB341](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB341)

## EGB342 — Telecommunications and RF
Analog/digital modulation for single/multi-user communications; RF fundamentals. Time/frequency domain signal analysis, information theory for digital data compression, modulation choice, performance analysis in noisy channels.
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB342](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EGB342)

## EFB335 — Investments
*School of Economics and Finance.* Investment environment and financial markets, asset classes and valuation techniques, portfolio theory and diversification, risk and return analysis, behavioural finance.
Source: [qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EFB335](https://www.qut.edu.au/study/unit?unitCode=EFB335)
