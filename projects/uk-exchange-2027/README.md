# UK Semester Exchange 2027

QUT semester abroad in the UK, Sept 2027 – Jan 2028, bookended by backpacking.

**Status:** Planning

## Description
A semester exchange at a QUT UK partner university during 2027 Semester 2 (Sept 2027 – Jan 2028), with roughly two months backpacking around Europe beforehand and possibly a month after before heading home. This project tracks university eligibility, the study plan needed for each candidate, and how it reconciles with the existing QUT degree plan (`context/degree-plan.md`).

## Key Dates
- Backpacking (pre-exchange): ~July–Aug 2027 (2 months, flexible)
- Study period: Sept 2027 – Jan 2028 (QUT's 2027 Semester 2)
- Backpacking (post-exchange, optional): ~Feb 2027... i.e. ~1 month after study period before returning home
- Application: universities require a minimum of 6 unit options per preference — the more approved before departure, the smoother registration at the host university
