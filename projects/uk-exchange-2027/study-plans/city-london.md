# City St George's, University of London — Candidate Units (Pass 1)

**Name change note:** "City University of London" merged and rebranded to **City St George's, University of London** (still the same institution QUT partners with). Use this name going forward for the application.

Exchange structure confirmed: **single semester (Autumn OR Spring) or full year**, both offered — Sept intake is the Autumn option, matches your window exactly. A full load is **4 modules**, which lines up exactly with the 4-unit exchange cap. Actual module lists for a given intake are only released via a request form, not fully public — a real limitation for this research pass.

Sources: [Study abroad programme](https://www.citystgeorges.ac.uk/prospective-students/apply/study-abroad/study-abroad-programme), [Bayes Business School undergraduate courses](https://www.bayes.citystgeorges.ac.uk/)

## Engineering side — likely disqualifying, not just under-researched
Confirmed directly (2026-08-03): **"The School of Science & Technology does not currently offer exchange opportunities"** at City St George's — and Electrical and Electronic Engineering sits within that School. This was found in the context of City's general cross-School "Study Abroad" (Freemover) programme, which explicitly lists only five other Schools (Business/Bayes, Communication & Creativity, Policy & Global Affairs, etc.) as participating.

**One open nuance:** City distinguishes between the **Partnership Programme** (for students nominated by a partner institution like QUT, via bilateral exchange agreement) and the **Freemover** cross-School Study Abroad programme. The "Science & Technology doesn't offer exchange" line was found describing the Freemover route specifically — it's not confirmed whether the same restriction applies to bilateral exchange nominees. **This needs a direct answer from QUT's exchange office or City's international office** — worth one email before ruling City out entirely, but the working assumption should now be that engineering is NOT accessible here.

Direct engineering course-page fetches also returned 403 throughout this research (site-wide bot-blocking, not necessarily related) — so no module-level content was recoverable regardless.

## Finance side — very strong, possibly the best of any university checked
Bayes Business School's **BSc Investment and Financial Risk Management** pathway modules cover, across Year 2/3:
- Asset management (investment performance measurement — mutual funds, hedge funds, ETFs) — strong match for **EFB335 Investments**
- Fixed income securities (valuation, return/volatility, bond portfolio management) — strong match for **EFB335 Investments**
- Corporate finance (company valuation, investment appraisal) — strong match for **EFB343 Corporate Finance**
- Asset allocation strategy / funding risk of financial institutions — strong match for **EFB344 Risk Management and Derivatives**

This single specialist pathway plausibly covers **three of the four swappable/fixed finance units** (EFB335, EFB343, EFB344) in one place — worth digging into module-level detail here first if City stays in contention, since it's the strongest finance signal found across any university so far. Also found: FR1101 "Finance and Investment" (Year 1 level, likely too basic).
