# University of Bristol — Candidate Units (Pass 1)

**Critical structural constraint:** Bristol runs Teaching Block 1 (TB1, ~Sept–Jan) and TB2 (~Jan–June). A Sept–Jan single-semester exchange student can only realistically take **TB1** units — several strong-looking modules are TB2-only or full-year (TB4) and are NOT usable for this visit. This is a much tighter filter than Aston, which didn't show this constraint.

Year 3 engineering/finance units require "strong background in the discipline" — should be fine given James's course load by 2027 S2. Some Masters-level (Year 3+) units are also listed as open to students with "3+ years study" — plausibly accessible given his overloaded progression.

Credit values not published in the study-abroad guide — general guidance is 60 Bristol credits/semester ≈ 3-4 units. Needs verifying in Pass 2 (affects how many modules are needed to hit the 48 QUT cp target).

Sources: [Electrical and Electronic Engineering study guide](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/centre-for-study-abroad/inbound/study-abroad-programmes-at-bristol/subjects-and-study-guides/electrical-and-electronic-engineering-study-guide/), [Accounting and Finance study guide](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/centre-for-study-abroad/inbound/study-abroad-programmes-at-bristol/subjects-and-study-guides/accounting-finance/)

## Read on the leftover-unit question
This is Bristol's weak spot: **the critical semester-locked unit (EGB345) has no match at all**, and EFB344/BSB399/EGH404 are also weak or TB2-locked. The units that *do* work well are EGB342 (Telecom/RF — strong, multiple options) and EFB335/EFB343 (Financial Markets/Corporate Finance/Behavioural Finance — strong). EGB348 and EGB341 are only partial.

If Bristol is pursued, the realistic 4-unit lineup would be something like: EGB342, EGB348 (Masters-level), EFB335 or EFB343, and one partial-match filler (EGB341) — **leaving EGB345 unmatched**, which is the exact scenario `../degree-plan-notes.md` flags as the hardest to absorb (its next free Sem-2 slot is already occupied by EGH446 in 2028 S2, and it gates EGH446/EGH445 downstream). Worth weighing this structural risk against Bristol's other advantages (reputation, city) before committing research time to a full Pass 2 here.
