## University of Sheffield (primary)

**VERIFIED against the real 2026/27 exchange module catalogue** (James found the actual published spreadsheet — full detail in `sheffield.md`).

The catalogue splits access into "subject-level exchange" (restricted to one subject/school only) and "university-wide exchange" (can mix schools). Since James needs a mixed Engineering + Finance load, he needs university-wide status — meaning modules reserved for subject-level-only students (several of the best engineering matches) aren't realistically accessible.

**Confirmed 4-unit semester lineup:**

| QUT Unit | Sheffield Module (verified, university-wide accessible) | Match |
|---|---|---|
| **EGB345** Control and Dynamic Systems | **ELE307 Feedback Systems Design** (Autumn, Level 3) | **Strong** |
| **EGB348** Electronics (fallback) | ELE203 Electric Circuits (Autumn, Level 2) | Weak — more basic than the ideal (reserved) match |
| EFB343 Corporate Finance | **MGT321 Advances in Corporate Finance** (Autumn) | **Strong** |
| EFB335 Investments | MGT3009 Company Analysis and Valuation (Autumn) | Good |
| EGB341 Energy Supply and Delivery | No accessible match (best option reserved for subject-level only) | None |
| EGB342 Telecommunications and RF | No accessible match (best option reserved for subject-level only) | None |
| EFB344 Risk Management and Derivatives | No Autumn match (best option, Financial Derivatives, is Spring-only) | None |
| BSB399, EGH404 | Nothing found | None |

**Full-year alternative** (if that path is chosen instead — see `sheffield.md`): Academic Year modules unlock much stronger matches for all 5 fixed units (ELE209 Control Systems Design and Analysis, ELE210 Electrical Energy Management and Conversion, ELE212 Signals/Systems/Communications, ELE220 Analogue and Digital Electronics, plus MGT3009/MGT321) — but this also means covering QUT's 2028 S1 units (EGH419, EGH404, EGH443, EGH448), which haven't been researched yet.

**Verdict:** Real, confirmed, viable 4-unit semester lineup. The cost: EGB341 and EGB342 both need rescheduling elsewhere in the degree — see `../degree-plan-notes.md` for the worked-out reshuffle. 12+ verified backup unit options (beyond the core 4) are listed in `sheffield.md` for the application's 6-minimum requirement.
