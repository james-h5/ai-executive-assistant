## Aston University

**Admin:** Sept–Jan single-semester exchange confirmed. Standard load = 60 Aston credits = 4 modules × 15cr = 48 QUT cp (matches the 4-unit cap exactly). No year-level restriction for Engineering or Business exchange students (Year 2 and Final Year both accessible).

| QUT Unit | Aston Candidate(s) | Match |
|---|---|---|
| **EGB345** Control and Dynamic Systems | Control Systems and Robotics (Y2) | **Strong** |
| **EGB348** Electronics | Analogue and Power Electronics (Y2) | Good |
| **EGB342** Telecommunications and RF | Signals and Systems (Y2), Communications Systems (Y2) + Digital Signal Processing, Digital/Optical Communications (Y3, backups) | **Strong**, good backups |
| **EGB341** Energy Supply and Delivery | Electrical Machines (Y2), Electric Motor Drives/Power Electronics (Y3) — all partial | **Weak** — no true grid/transmission match |
| **EFB335** Investments | Portfolio Management and Behavioural Finance (Final Yr) + Financial Markets, Trading Strategies, Derivatives, Financial Analysis (backups) | **Strong**, excellent backups |
| EFB343 Corporate Finance | Corporate Finance (Y2), Advanced Corporate Finance (Final Yr) | Strong |
| EFB344 Risk Management and Derivatives | Derivatives, Further Topics in Derivatives (Final Yr) | Strong |
| BSB399 Business Capstone | Nothing found | None |
| EGH404 Research in Engineering Practice | Professional Engineering Practice (partial), Final Year Project (too broad) | Weak |

**Verdict:** 4 of the original 5 fixed units already match well (EGB345, EGB348, EGB342, EFB335) — a clean 4-unit exchange semester without touching the swap pool. Only EGB341 has no good match; cleanest fix is relocating the overload to 2027 S1 or 2029 S1 rather than adding an extra semester (see `../degree-plan-notes.md`).

## University of Sheffield

**UPDATE (verified against the real 2026/27 exchange module catalogue — see `sheffield.md` for full detail):** the earlier Pass 1 findings below were based on Sheffield's general degree course page and turned out to name modules that don't actually match the real exchange catalogue (wrong codes, wrong department, or not semester-accessible). **Critical open question:** the real catalogue splits exchange access into "subject to subject exchange students" and "university wide exchange students" columns — several of the best engineering matches are only open to the former.
