# Top 4 Candidates — Unit Comparison Summary

## Aston University

**Admin:** Sept–Jan single-semester exchange confirmed. Standard load = 60 Aston credits = 4 modules × 15cr = 48 QUT cp (matches the 4-unit cap exactly). No year-level restriction for Engineering or Business exchange students (Year 2 and Final Year both accessible).

| QUT Unit | Aston Candidate(s) | Match |
|---|---|---|
| **EGB345** Control and Dynamic Systems | Control Systems and Robotics (Y2) | **Strong** |
| **EGB348** Electronics | Analogue and Power Electronics (Y2) | Good |
| **EGB342** Telecommunications and RF | Signals and Systems (Y2), Communications Systems (Y2) + Digital Signal Processing, Digital/Optical Communications (Y3, backups) | **Strong**, good backups |
| **EGB341** Energy Supply and Delivery | Electrical Machines (Y2), Electric Motor Drives/Power Electronics (Y3) — all partial | **Weak** — no true grid/transmission match |
| **EFB335** Investments | Portfolio Management and Behavioural Finance (Final Yr) + Financial Markets, Trading Strategies, Derivatives, Financial Analysis (backups) | **Strong**, excellent backups |
| EFB343 Corporate Finance | Corporate Finance (Y2), Advanced Corporate Finance (Final Yr) | Strong |
| EFB344 Risk Management and Derivatives | Derivatives, Further Topics in Derivatives (Final Yr) | Strong |
| BSB399 Business Capstone | Nothing found | None |
| EGH404 Research in Engineering Practice | Professional Engineering Practice (partial), Final Year Project (too broad) | Weak |

**Verdict:** 4 of the original 5 fixed units already match well (EGB345, EGB348, EGB342, EFB335) — a clean 4-unit exchange semester without touching the swap pool. Only EGB341 has no good match; cleanest fix is relocating the overload to 2027 S1 or 2029 S1 rather than adding an extra semester (see `../degree-plan-notes.md`).

## University of Birmingham

**Admin:** Engineering confirmed accessible for single-semester exchange (Chemical Engineering is the one explicitly full-year-only exception — Electrical/Electronic isn't excluded). Logistics note: single-semester Engineering students may sit summative assessment in the January or May/June period at Birmingham, or have it invigilated at QUT — needs coordinating, not a blocker. Business School has no semester restriction, just a standard English-language threshold.

| QUT Unit | Birmingham Candidate(s) | Match |
|---|---|---|

| EGH404 Research in Engineering Practice | Nothing found | None |

**Verdict:** Solid 3rd choice — strong telecom/electronics, and a genuinely good standalone power-systems option (better than Aston's or Bristol's equivalent). Weak spot mirrors Manchester Met: EGB345 is bundled into a combined control+power module rather than getting its own — needs the actual syllabus to judge if it's deep enough.

## City St George's, University of London

*(formerly "City, University of London" — same institution, renamed after a 2024 merger with St George's)*

**Admin:** Autumn or Spring single semester available (Sept intake = Autumn, matches your window), full load = 4 modules (matches the cap exactly).

**Critical finding:** City's **School of Science & Technology (houses Electrical/Electronic Engineering) "does not currently offer exchange opportunities"** — confirmed for the general cross-School Study Abroad (Freemover) programme. Not yet confirmed whether this also applies to bilateral Partnership Programme exchange students (which is what a QUT nomination would be) — **one direct question to QUT's exchange office or City's international office settles this.**

| QUT Unit | City Candidate(s) | Match |
|---|---|---|
| EGB345, EGB348, EGB341, EGB342 | Unresearched — engineering course pages returned 403 throughout, and access itself is now in question | **N/A** |

| EFB344 Risk Management and Derivatives | Same pathway: asset allocation strategy / funding risk of financial institutions | Strong |

**Verdict:** Best finance-side match of any of the 4 — one specialist pathway plausibly covers three finance units at once. But moot if engineering access is genuinely blocked, since James needs both sides matched. **Low priority until the Partnership Programme question is answered.**
