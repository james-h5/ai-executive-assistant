## Reading across all 6
- **Sheffield comes out strongest on engineering** — real, well-named Year 3 modules for control systems, power systems, and communications, plus a clean semester structure. Biggest open item: confirming cross-pathway module mixing and exact semester timing.
- **Aston is the safest all-rounder** — nothing spectacular, but no major red flags either, and the only one already at Pass-1-complete confidence on both sides.
- **City has the best finance story** but is a blind spot on engineering — worth a direct follow-up (module request form) before ruling in or out, since the finance side alone can't carry a mixed Eng/Finance exchange semester.
- **Bristol and Leeds share the same weakness**: no confirmed match for EGB345, the one unit we most need to get right (semester-locked, gates EGH446/EGH445). Both also have real structural friction (Bristol's TB1/TB2 split, Leeds' full-year-module pattern) that's worse than Aston or Sheffield.
- **Manchester Met is the least-resolved** — not because it looks bad, but because public sourcing was thin across the board (no semester data at all, ambiguous module scopes).

## Suggested next move
Given this spread, worth prioritizing **Sheffield and Aston** for Pass 2 (detailed syllabus comparison + semester confirmation) since they have the strongest EGB345 matches and no major structural red flags. City is worth one more targeted push (the module request form) specifically to resolve the engineering blind spot, since its finance side is too good to write off without checking. Bristol/Leeds/Manchester Met could stay on the shortlist as backups (QUT wants 6 unit options minimum per preference anyway) but probably don't need more research time unless the top 3 fall through.
