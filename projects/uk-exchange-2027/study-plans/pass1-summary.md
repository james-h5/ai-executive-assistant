## Reading across all 7
- **Sheffield comes out strongest on engineering** — real, well-named Year 3 modules for control systems, power systems, and communications, plus a clean semester structure. Biggest open item: confirming cross-pathway module mixing and exact semester timing.
- **Aston is the safest all-rounder** — nothing spectacular, but no major red flags either, and the only one already at Pass-1-complete confidence on both sides.
- **City is likely disqualified, not just under-researched**: confirmed City's School of Science & Technology (which houses Electrical/Electronic Engineering) "does not currently offer exchange opportunities" under the general Study Abroad programme. One nuance left unresolved — this was found describing the Freemover route specifically, and it's not confirmed whether QUT's bilateral Partnership exchange is treated differently. Downgraded to low priority; the excellent finance side can't be used alone since James needs engineering matched too.
- **Birmingham (added 2026-08-03) is a solid 4th** — confirmed single-semester-accessible for Engineering, strong telecom/electronics matches, and a genuinely good standalone Power Electronics and Power Systems module for EGB341. Its weak spot: EGB345 (control) is only found bundled into a combined "Electrical Energy Systems and Control" module, no standalone match yet — needs the syllabus to judge if that's deep enough.
- **Bristol and Leeds share the same weakness**: no confirmed match for EGB345. Both also have real structural friction (Bristol's TB1/TB2 split, Leeds' full-year-module pattern) that's worse than Aston, Sheffield, or Birmingham.
- **Manchester Met is the least-resolved** — not because it looks bad, but because public sourcing was thin across the board (no semester data at all, ambiguous module scopes).

## Suggested next move
Priority order for Pass 2 (detailed syllabus comparison + semester confirmation): **Sheffield, Aston, then Birmingham** — all three have real, mostly-standalone matches and no disqualifying structural issues. City needs exactly one direct question (to QUT's exchange office or City's international office) about whether Partnership-Programme exchange students can access Science & Technology despite the general exclusion — cheap to ask, decides the whole university one way or the other. Bristol/Leeds/Manchester Met can stay as backups for the QUT-required 6-option minimum but don't need more research time unless the top 3 fall through.
