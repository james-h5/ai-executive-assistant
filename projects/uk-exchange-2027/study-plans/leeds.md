# University of Leeds — Candidate Units (Pass 1)

**Bigger structural risk than Aston or Bristol.** Leeds runs Semester 1 (Sept–Jan) / Semester 2, but unlike Bristol's clean TB1/TB2 split, a meaningful chunk of Leeds modules — in *both* Engineering and Business School — run **full year** rather than single-semester. General guidance found: "Electronic and Electrical Engineering (ELEC) teaches the majority of their modules across the full academic year" and single-semester exchange students "would have significant limitations" in ELEC specifically. Confirmed directly in the catalogue: ELEC2101 (Electronics) and LUBS2206 (Corporate Finance) are both full-year — good content matches, unusable for a single semester.

Module catalogue: [catalogue.leeds.ac.uk](https://catalogue.leeds.ac.uk) — filter "Undergraduate modules" + "search by incoming Study Abroad" for 'isa'-flagged modules. Standard exchange load: apply for 50-100 credits/semester as backups (a 20-credit Leeds module = 10 ECTS). Sources: [Academic subject requirements](https://www.leeds.ac.uk/international-exchange-and-study-abroad/doc/check-academic-subject-requirements), [What can I study?](https://www.leeds.ac.uk/international-exchange-and-study-abroad/doc/can-i-study)

## Against EGB345 — Control and Dynamic Systems
**No Semester-1 match found.** Same gap as Bristol — no standalone control-systems module surfaced as Semester 1 in the searches done so far. Given the "ELEC mostly full-year" pattern, this may well be a full-year-only module here too. **This is the critical unit (semester-locked, gates EGH446/EGH445) — needs direct confirmation with Leeds before ruling Leeds in or out.**

## Against EGB348 — Electronics
- Electronic Circuits and Systems Design (ELEC2101, 20cr, Year 2) — good content match (signal conditioning, amplifiers, ADC, digital interfacing) but **full year — not usable for a single semester**

## Against EGB341 — Energy Supply and Delivery
- **Electric Power Generation and Distribution** (ELEC5580M, 15cr, **Semester 1**, M-level) — **excellent match**, best found across any university so far: covers power/energy system layers, components, operation, technical+economic aspects, low-carbon transition. M-level code (5xxx) — needs confirming this is genuinely accessible to an undergraduate exchange student, and not flagged against "discovery module" restrictions noted in the catalogue entry.

## Against EGB342 — Telecommunications and RF
- **Wireless Communications Systems Design** (ELEC5333M, 15cr, **Semester 1**) — strong match: physical layer of wireless comms, industrial design software, lab work

## Against EFB335 / EFB343 (Investments / Corporate Finance)
- Corporate Financial Management (LUBS2206, 20cr, Level 2) — strong content match for EFB343 (capital structure, CAPM, EMH, behavioural finance, M&A) but **full year — not usable for a single semester**
- Banking and Finance BSc (general programme, not yet unit-level confirmed) — mentions compulsory "investment banking, risk management" plus optional "behavioural finance, portfolio management, financial derivatives" — plausible matches for EFB335/EFB344 but need specific module codes + semester confirmation in Pass 2
