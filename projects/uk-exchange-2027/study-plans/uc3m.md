# Universidad Carlos III de Madrid (UC3M): Candidate Units

Added 2026-10-27 to replace Aston. Spain isn't England, so UC3M can go in any preference slot.

**Source:** UC3M's official bachelor degree study-programme pages. Each course row has a language flag (English and/or Spanish). Course content comes from each course's official sheet (2026/27 version). Courses are pulled from 3 degrees, all run by the Escuela Politécnica Superior (Leganés campus):
- Industrial Electronics and Automation Engineering (IEAE): [uc3m.es/bachelor-degree/electronic](https://www.uc3m.es/bachelor-degree/electronic)
- Electrical Power Engineering (EPE): [uc3m.es/bachelor-degree/electrical](https://www.uc3m.es/bachelor-degree/electrical)
- Telecommunication Technologies Engineering (TTE): [uc3m.es/bachelor-degree/telecommunication](https://www.uc3m.es/bachelor-degree/telecommunication)

**Structure:** 6 ECTS courses. Semester 1 runs Sept–Dec with exams in Jan. Semester 2 runs Feb–May with exams in June. A full year is 60 ECTS, which is 10 courses.

**English-only filter:** every course below has an English group. Many of the most relevant power courses are **Spanish-only** and are excluded: Electric Power Systems, HV Power Lines and Substations, Power Stations, Protection, Industrial Electronics, Microprocessors and Microcontrollers (IEAE).

### Backups (all English-taught)
- **EGB240:** Microprocessor Based Digital Systems (15380, TTE Y3 S1). Covers microcontroller I/O, ADC/DAC, interrupts, timers, serial comms, assembler. **This could beat Digital Electronics if EGB240 is embedded-heavy.** Second backup: Electronic Instrumentation (18419, IEAE Y3 S2), covering signal conditioning, instrumentation amps, sensors, ADC/DAC.
- **EGB348:** Electronic Components and Circuits (15374, TTE Y2 S1); Electronic Systems (15383, TTE Y3 S2); Integrated Circuits and Microelectronics (15389, TTE Y3 S2)
- **EGB342:** Digital Communications (15385, TTE 2019 plan Y3 S1). Covers PAM, optimal receivers, ISI and equalisation, multicarrier, spread spectrum
- **EGB241:** Magnetic Circuits and Transformers (13984, EPE 2019 plan Y3 S1). Same language risk as Rotating Machines
- **EGB341:** Fundamentals of Transient Phenomena in Power Grids (18407, EPE Y2 S1, 3 ECTS); Electrical Power Engineering Fundamentals (13977, EPE Y2 S1). The second is three-phase and power basics, probably too introductory for the 80% bar

## Risks / things to confirm

1. **EGB241 machines: the English group is being phased out.** EPE is moving from its 2019 plan to a new 2025 plan. In the 2019 plan, Rotating Machines and Magnetic Circuits and Transformers have English groups. In the new plan, both are Spanish-only. By 2027/28, Year 3 will probably be on the new plan. **If so, there's no English machines course, and EGB241 falls back to EM Fields alone, which only covers half the unit.** Ask UC3M directly.
2. **EGB341 is still the weakest unit.** The real transmission/distribution course (Electric Power Systems) is Spanish-only. CAD Power System Modelling is a good substitute on content, but it's a 4th-year elective, so it isn't guaranteed to run. This is the same EGB341 gap seen at Sheffield, Aston and Strathclyde.
3. **Mixing courses across 3 degrees.** All 3 degrees are run by the same school, but confirm exchange students can enrol across them and get a seat in the English groups. English groups have capped places.
4. **Credit reconciliation.** 60 ECTS is roughly 96 QUT cp (8 units), but this plan covers 6 QUT units, so each unit is matched by 2 courses. This is the same open "6 vs 8" question flagged in `sheffield.md`. Ask QUT whether 2 × 6 ECTS can count toward one 12cp unit, or whether the extra courses need separate matches.
5. **Language of the application itself.** UC3M degree students need B2 English. Exchange students taking English groups should need no Spanish, but check UC3M's incoming exchange requirements.
