# Europe Bucket List

A reference list of places worth seeing across Europe — not an itinerary, no dates or durations attached. Weighted toward history and culture (ruins, old towns, castles, museums) since that's the draw, with standout nature, beach, and nightlife spots mixed in per country. Pull from this once real backpacking dates and a route start taking shape.

**Not included:** the UK itself. James is based there for the exchange (see `university-shortlist.md` for weekend-Europe-access notes by host city) — this list is about destinations, not the home base.

## Western Europe

### France
- Paris — Louvre, Eiffel Tower, Notre-Dame, Montmartre
- Palace of Versailles
- Normandy — D-Day beaches, Mont Saint-Michel
- Loire Valley châteaux
- Provence — Avignon, Roman ruins at Pont du Gard, lavender fields (if timing lines up)
- French Riviera — Nice, Cannes, Monaco nightlife

### Belgium
- Bruges — medieval canals and old town
- Ghent
- Brussels — Grand Place, EU quarter
- Ypres — Flanders Fields WWI battlefields and memorials
- Antwerp nightlife

### Netherlands
- Amsterdam — canals, Anne Frank House, Rijksmuseum, nightlife
- Keukenhof tulip fields (spring only)
- Giethoorn — car-free canal village
- Rotterdam — modern architecture contrast

### Luxembourg
- Luxembourg City — old town and fortifications (UNESCO)
- Vianden Castle

### Germany
- Berlin — Berlin Wall remnants, Brandenburg Gate, museums, nightlife
- Neuschwanstein Castle
- Munich — Marienplatz, beer halls (Oktoberfest if timing lines up)
- Cologne Cathedral
- Rhine Valley castles (river cruise between Koblenz and Mainz)
- Dachau memorial site
- Hamburg nightlife

### Switzerland
- Swiss Alps — Jungfraujoch, Matterhorn/Zermatt
- Lucerne old town and lake
- Interlaken — adventure sports base (skydiving, paragliding — fits the bucket list)
- Geneva

### Austria
- Vienna — Schönbrunn Palace, Hofburg, opera house
- Salzburg — Mozart's birthplace, Sound of Music filming sites
- Hallstatt — lakeside alpine village
- Innsbruck — Alps access

### Ireland
- Dublin — Guinness Storehouse, Trinity College, pub nightlife (Temple Bar)
- Cliffs of Moher
- Ring of Kerry
- Giant's Causeway (technically Northern Ireland, usually done as one trip)

## Southern Europe

### Italy
- Rome — Colosseum, Roman Forum, Vatican/Sistine Chapel, Pantheon
- Florence — Uffizi Gallery, Duomo, Renaissance core
- Venice — canals, St Mark's Square
- Pompeii and Herculaneum
- Amalfi Coast
- Cinque Terre
- Sicily — Palermo, Mt Etna, Valley of the Temples
- Pisa (Leaning Tower — quick stop)

### Spain
- Barcelona — Sagrada Família, Gaudí architecture, nightlife
- Madrid — Prado Museum, Royal Palace
- Seville — Alcázar, flamenco
- Granada — Alhambra
- San Sebastián — Basque coast
- Ibiza nightlife
- Toledo — day trip from Madrid

### Portugal
- Lisbon — Belém Tower, historic trams, nightlife (Bairro Alto)
- Porto — port wine cellars
- Sintra — palaces (Pena Palace)
- Algarve — cliffs and beaches

### Greece
- Athens — Acropolis, Parthenon
- Santorini
- Mykonos nightlife
- Delphi
- Crete — Knossos ruins
- Meteora — cliff-top monasteries

### Malta
- Valletta old town
- Mdina — "Silent City"
- Blue Lagoon, Comino
- Ħal Saflieni Hypogeum / megalithic temples

## Central Europe

### Czech Republic
- Prague — Charles Bridge, Prague Castle, Old Town, nightlife
- Český Krumlov
- Kutná Hora — Sedlec Ossuary (Bone Church)

### Poland
- Kraków — old town, Wawel Castle
- Auschwitz-Birkenau
- Warsaw — rebuilt Old Town, WWII history
- Wieliczka Salt Mine
- Zakopane / Tatra Mountains

### Hungary
- Budapest — Parliament building, Fisherman's Bastion, thermal baths, ruin bar nightlife
- Lake Balaton

### Slovakia
- Bratislava old town
- High Tatras

### Slovenia
- Ljubljana
- Lake Bled
- Postojna Cave / Predjama Castle
- Piran — coastal old town

## Balkans & Eastern Europe

### Croatia
- Dubrovnik — old city walls
- Split — Diocletian's Palace
- Plitvice Lakes National Park
- Hvar — island nightlife
- Zagreb

### Bosnia and Herzegovina
- Mostar — Stari Most bridge
- Sarajevo — Latin Bridge (Franz Ferdinand assassination site), siege history, old bazaar

### Serbia
- Belgrade — fortress, Skadarlija old quarter, river-barge nightlife
- Novi Sad

### Montenegro
- Kotor — old town and bay
- Budva — beaches and nightlife

### Albania
- Berat and Gjirokastër — Ottoman-era old towns
- Albanian Riviera beaches

### Romania
- Bran Castle ("Dracula's Castle")
- Bucharest
- Transylvania — Sighișoara, Brașov
- Carpathian Mountains

### Bulgaria
- Sofia
- Plovdiv old town
- Rila Monastery
- Varna — Black Sea coast nightlife

## Scandinavia & Nordics

### Norway
- Fjords — Geirangerfjord, Nærøyfjord
- Lofoten Islands
- Bergen
- Tromsø — Northern Lights (winter)

### Sweden
- Stockholm — Gamla Stan old town, Vasa Museum
- Gothenburg

### Denmark
- Copenhagen — Nyhavn, Tivoli Gardens, nightlife
- Legoland (Billund)

### Finland
- Helsinki
- Lapland — Northern Lights, Santa Claus Village

### Iceland
- Reykjavik nightlife
- Golden Circle — geysers, waterfalls, Þingvellir
- Blue Lagoon
- Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon

## Baltics

### Estonia
- Tallinn old town

### Latvia
- Riga old town and nightlife

### Lithuania
- Vilnius old town
- Curonian Spit

## Turkey

- Istanbul — Hagia Sophia, Blue Mosque, Grand Bazaar, Bosphorus
- Cappadocia — hot air balloons, fairy chimney rock formations
- Ephesus — ancient ruins
- Pamukkale — travertine terraces
- Antalya / Turkish Riviera beaches
