# Making the case for a full year

## The pitch, in one line
The academic case for a full year at Sheffield is actually *stronger* than the semester (all 9 QUT units across both displaced semesters have real matches, vs. 2 needing rework for a semester alone), the cost gap is smaller than a naive "double the money" assumption, and none of it touches the mid-2029 graduation target.

## The money conversation, specifically (this is where it'll actually go)

You already know the shape of it: he asks where the money's coming from, you say the OS-HELP loan, he says you still have to pay that off, you say you'll try get a job. Here's what to have cold for the exchange after that, since "I'll try get a job" on its own won't land as a real answer — it needs a number behind it.

**The full picture, worst-case:**
| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Full year total cost (lean budget, worst-case ceiling) | ~$31,500 AUD |
| Minus savings already in the bank | −$9,300 |
| Minus OS-HELP (two loans — full year is two separate 6-month periods) | −$16,884 |
| **Gap remaining, before you've earned a single dollar over there** | **~$5,300 AUD** |

That's the number to lead with, not "I'll get a job" in the abstract — **you're already ~83% funded before any UK income**, and the remaining ~$5,300 is what a part-time bar job over there is closing, not the whole trip. That reframes it from "I'm hoping it works out" to "the gap I actually need to cover is small and specific."

**On "you still have to pay that off" — have these three facts ready:**
- OS-HELP debt isn't like a normal loan — no real interest, just indexed to inflation/wages (whichever is lower), and it can't be called in or demand a fixed monthly payment.
- It's repaid automatically through the tax system, only once you're earning above $69,528/year, as a percentage of income above that line — not a bill that shows up.
- You're going to carry HECS debt from the degree regardless of this trip (realistically $60-80k by graduation just from the double degree). The $16,884 OS-HELP is roughly a 20-25% add-on to debt you're already taking on either way — not a new, separate financial burden category.

**On the job specifically, if he pushes on "what if you can't get one":**
- British passport means no visa or hours restriction — you're not competing with international students capped at 20hrs/week.
- You've already got real bartending experience, which is exactly the kind of work a 65,000-student city like Sheffield has constant demand for.
- But keep it honest: the job is what shrinks the ~$5,300 gap further and builds a buffer — it was never the thing making the trip possible in the first place. The savings + loan already do that.

## Concern → answer

**"Can you actually afford this without needing bailout money from us?"**
Budget worked out in detail: full year (2.5mo backpacking + ~10mo Sheffield, including flights and buffer) runs **~$25,000–31,500 AUD worst-case**. Against that: $9,300 already saved, OS-HELP covers up to $16,884 (two loans, since a full year is two 6-month study periods), and UK work is legally unrestricted (British passport, no visa needed) as a backstop if the number needs closing further. This isn't "hope it works out" — it's a real number with real funding sources identified.

**"Won't this push back your degree?"**
Checked directly against Sheffield's real 2026/27 exchange module catalogue — not just the general course pages. All 9 QUT units spanning the two semesters a full year would displace (2027 S2 + 2028 S1) have either a strong or workable match, except one (EGH404, Research in Engineering Practice) with no equivalent found. That one unit has no semester-lock and no downstream unit depending on it, so it just gets rescheduled as a normal overload addition elsewhere — same mechanism already used once this degree (EGB242 was pulled forward from 2027 S1 into 2026 S2 without issue). Mid-2029 finish date holds.

**"Is Sheffield safe, and what if something goes wrong while you're there?"**
UK is a stable, English-speaking country. As a British citizen, full legal residency rights apply — no visa, no immigration risk, and NHS healthcare access. Sheffield specifically has 65,000+ students across two universities, meaning huge built-in infrastructure (university welfare services, an international student community from 150+ countries, a Students' Union rated #1 in the UK).

**"What if you need us and you're on the other side of the world?"**
Video calls make this a smaller gap than it used to be, and a full year (vs. a rushed semester) means more time to actually build a real support network there rather than being isolated — course mates, the university boxing club, other students. Worth agreeing a regular call schedule up front so it's a habit, not an afterthought.

**"Why not just do the semester — isn't that the safer option?"**
It looks safer on the surface, but the semester actually has a rougher academic fit (only 2 of 4 core engineering units get a real match, two need rescheduling elsewhere in the degree) and the cost difference to the full year is smaller than "half the price" once OS-HELP and UK work are factored in. The full year isn't reckless ambition — on the numbers, it's arguably the cleaner outcome.

## Precautions worth having in place (regardless of how the conversation goes)

1. **Separate emergency buffer** — a few hundred pounds kept untouched, distinct from the weekly travel/living budget, for the "flight got cancelled" or "lost a bag" moment.
2. **Comprehensive travel insurance** for the full period, including the backpacking leg specifically — NHS access covers UK healthcare, but doesn't cover the 2.5 months in continental Europe, lost luggage, or trip cancellation.
3. **Regular check-in schedule agreed in advance** — especially during the unstructured backpacking weeks, since that's the part with the least built-in structure. A simple weekly call plus location-sharing during the backpacking leg covers most of the "what if we can't reach you" anxiety.
4. **Faculty unit approval locked in before departure** — get the direct-match units formally signed off by QUT's faculty ahead of time, with the backup options already identified (see `study-plans/sheffield.md`) ready if a module clashes or gets cancelled once there.
5. **Passport validity check** — both Australian and British passports need to comfortably cover the full trip length plus a buffer, not just expire partway through.
6. **GP registration and prescriptions sorted** in the first week in Sheffield, not left until something's needed.
7. **Know the money-access path from Australia** — a way to move funds quickly if the budget needs topping up (bank transfer set up in advance, not figured out under pressure).
