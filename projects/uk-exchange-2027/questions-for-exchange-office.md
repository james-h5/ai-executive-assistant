# Questions for the Exchange Office

Compiled from everything that came up as a genuine open item across the university shortlist, Sheffield deep-dive, and semester-vs-year comparison. Organised so related questions land together — send as one email or split by recipient (QUT vs Sheffield) as needed.

## 1. Application mechanics & timing
1. When do I need to finalise semester-length (1 semester vs full year) and my university preference ranking? Is there a hard deadline before which this can't be changed?
2. How many university preferences do I rank on the application, and how does the fallback process work if my first choice isn't available or falls through?
3. For each preferred university, I understand I need a minimum of 6 approved unit options — does approval happen before I depart, and what's the process/timeline for getting faculty sign-off on the specific units I've identified?
4. Would a real, published module catalogue from the host university (e.g. Sheffield's own "Study Abroad & Exchange Modules Catalogue") count as sufficient documentation for faculty approval, or do you need something more formal (official unit outlines/syllabi in a specific format)?

## 2. Sheffield-specific (send to Sheffield's Global Opportunities office, or ask QUT to clarify on my behalf)
5. Is QUT's exchange partnership with Sheffield classified as "university-wide exchange" (application code VISU02/VIST53) or "subject-level exchange" (VISU01/VIST37)? This determines which modules I can actually access, per Sheffield's own exchange module catalogue.
6. Given I'll need a mixed Engineering + Management/Economics course load, can you confirm university-wide exchange students are able to select modules across those two schools without restriction?
7. For Level 3 (300-level) Electrical and Electronic Engineering modules (e.g. ELE307 Feedback Systems Design), what documentation of "prior undergraduate background" is required, and would completed QUT units (e.g. EGB242) satisfy that requirement?
8. Can you confirm the semester dates for 2027/28 (Autumn and Spring) precisely, so I can check alignment with QUT's own semester calendar?

## 3. Credit conversion & study load
9. What's the exact credit conversion QUT uses for Sheffield specifically — is it a flat 12 QUT cp = 15 UK credits, or does it vary by host institution/module size (Sheffield uses a mix of 10cr, 15cr, and 20cr modules, not a uniform 15cr)?
10. Does a single Sheffield Autumn semester (60 Sheffield credits) satisfy QUT's full-time study-abroad requirement on its own, or is there a minimum QUT-cp threshold I need to hit regardless of how host credits convert?

## 4. Which QUT semester(s) get displaced
11. For a single Sept–Jan exchange semester, can you confirm this is treated as equivalent to QUT's 2027 Semester 2 specifically (given the UK semester runs later into January than QUT's S2, which usually finishes in November)?
12. For a full academic year exchange (Sept 2027–June 2028), can you confirm this would be treated as covering QUT's **2027 Semester 2 and 2028 Semester 1** (not 2027 S1+S2), given the UK's Spring semester overlaps QUT's 2028 S1 calendar?

## 5. Degree-plan reshuffle & overload rules
13. If one or two of my currently-planned 2027 S2 units (e.g. EGB341 Energy Supply and Delivery) don't have a matching unit at my host university, can I reschedule them into a later semester as an added overload unit (5th unit in an otherwise-standard semester)? Is that routinely approved, or does it need special faculty sign-off each time?
14. Is there a cap on how much overload the enrolment system allows per semester, separate from the general "60cp is a lot" guidance?
15. I've already pulled one unit forward once (EGB242, from 2027 S1 into 2026 S2) — is there a limit to how many times units can be reshuffled like this across my remaining degree plan?

## 6. GPA-dependent eligibility
16. My cumulative GPA is currently 5.71 (through 2026 S1); 2026 S2 results aren't out yet. King's College London requires a 5.8 GPA. By what date does my GPA need to meet that threshold for King's to be a valid preference — does it need to be met at time of application, or is there flexibility if results land after I've submitted preferences?

## 7. OS-HELP
17. Can you confirm my OS-HELP eligibility (Commonwealth Supported Place status, credit point minimum) based on my current progress?
18. For a full academic year exchange (two consecutive 6-month periods), can I actually draw two separate OS-HELP loans, or is a 12-month continuous exchange treated as a single loan period for OS-HELP purposes? The rules say "one loan per six-month study period, two loans lifetime" but I want to confirm a full year genuinely qualifies for two, not one.
19. What are the current (or best available projection for 2027/28) OS-HELP loan amounts for study outside Asia?

## 8. Citizenship-specific
20. I hold a British passport alongside my Australian citizenship — does this change any part of the QUT exchange application process, or any documentation/registration I need to complete with QUT specifically (separate from the fact that I won't need a UK student visa)?
